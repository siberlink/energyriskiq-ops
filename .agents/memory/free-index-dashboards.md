---
name: Free index dashboards
description: Entitlement policy for the three core risk-index dashboards.
---

Authenticated accounts on the Free plan receive the complete GERI, EERI, and EGSI dashboard experience, including the full dashboard modules and available index history. This policy is specific to those dashboards and does not grant access to separate paid products such as GERI Live.

**Why:** The three index dashboards are the core intelligence experience promised to Free users; partial client-side plan gates caused account holders to see only abbreviated data despite the underlying dashboard data being available.

**How to apply:** When changing GERI, EERI, or EGSI dashboard behavior, keep frontend visibility rules, dashboard API response limits, and authenticated trader-intelligence paths consistent for Free users. Preserve separate entitlements for standalone paid features.
